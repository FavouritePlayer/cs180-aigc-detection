# Frontier 100-image pilot: MoGe-2 versus Marigold

Frozen before inference. Dataset: `wasitaigeneratedcom/frontier-image-video-bench-2026-10`, revision `e6a8b83d15fab1da958848368fcd07e943afafd0`. Public original images only; no videos or social copies. Exactly 50 real, 50 AI images. Deterministic SHA-256 ordering with seed `cs180-frontier-2026-10-pilot-v1`; real photos selected by hash, AI images selected round-robin across all 19 generators (2–3 per generator). Selection never uses estimator scores or visual judgments. Per-file labels, provenance, licenses and hashes are in `frontier_manifest.json`.

Models: MoGe-2 Large (`Ruicheng/moge-2-vitl`, same verified checkpoint/source as stage 1 round 2) and Marigold Normals v1.1 (`prs-eth/marigold-normals-v1-1`, revision `09cfdd258cb281fa006cf1afcd2284376d16687d`, same verified fp16 weights as stage 1). Marigold uses 4 DDIM steps and ensemble 10, processing resolution 768, matching input resolution, deterministic image-ID seed. MoGe uses resolution level 9, official fp16 inference, learned metric scale, no supplied FOV, `apply_mask=False`. No ground truth depth, normals, intrinsics or EXIF input.

Preprocessing: RGB, no crop, aspect-preserving resize to longest edge exactly 1024 using PIL LANCZOS (including upsampling if needed). Both estimators receive identical pixels. A shared canvas avoids systematic differences in score stencil size caused by image resolution. Original and processed-pixel hashes are retained. Labels are used only after scores are produced.

Geometry: convert MoGe's axial depth to camera-space points using MoGe's predicted normalized intrinsics converted to pixels. Orient depth-derived normals toward the camera and express them in Marigold's right/up/toward-viewer convention. No fitted rotation, sign, scale, or offset. Compute central differences with radii 4 (primary) and 1 (secondary) canvas pixels. Compare with independently predicted Marigold normals by oriented angular error. Exclude only the stencil border and nonfinite/nonpositive/degenerate predictions; report coverage and failures. Do not use MoGe's learned validity mask to filter the primary score. Model-mask coverage is diagnostic. Do not discard images based on scores; any failed images must be reported.

Primary image score: mean angular disagreement at radius 4, in degrees. Higher disagreement is fixed in advance as indicating AI generation. Primary outcome: AUROC across all 100 images; do not flip the score direction if AUROC is below 0.5. Report 95% stratified image-bootstrap interval (2000 replicates, seed 180), class mean/median distributions and per-image scores. Radius 1, median angular score and mean absolute camera-facing component difference are secondary diagnostics, not candidates from which to choose a better headline. No trained classifier or test-set optimized threshold/accuracy. With 50 real images, empirical FPR steps are 2%; this pilot cannot establish performance at 1% FPR. Generator-specific samples are too small for firm rankings. The balanced generator sampling describes this pilot, not the benchmark's full natural mixture or private leaderboard.

This tests a proposed detection signal, not reference depth/normal accuracy. Both estimators can agree on a plausible but incorrect reconstruction, or disagree on a real image. Content/source, compression, rendering style and camera-estimation errors remain possible confounders. Results are exploratory; this is not evidence of broad generalization without further controlled, held-out testing.
